01 — What EduRank is03

A school ecosystem for sharing notes and climbing the ranks.

EduRank lets South African high-school students (Grades 8–12) upload the notes that helped them, earn points for sharing, and spend those points to unlock other students’ work — wrapped in a leaderboard, a school map, and a free library of official notes.

The problem it solves

Notes and past-paper-style study material already get traded between students on WhatsApp and at break time — informally, unfairly, and with no reward for the person who made it. EduRank gives that exchange a proper home: one place to share, earn, and be recognised.

Current status

  • Live at edurank.co.za, served from Cloudflare’s global network.
  • Fresh start: accounts were cleared, so the site invites its first real users (“Be the first”).
  • 25 official notes already published for Grades 11–12 in Mathematics, Physical Sciences, Life Sciences and Accounting.
  • You (the owner) are the only account with full visibility.

02 — How it works04

The student’s journey, step by step.

01
Sign up. Name, email, password — with a quick “I’m human” check (Turnstile) and an email confirmation link.
02
Pick a grade. This locks for the school year, so a Grade 11 student only ever sees Grade 11 material.
03
Pick a school on the map. Tap the school on the map of South Africa; it locks once chosen and shows on the leaderboards.
04
Browse & read. Free notes open in the browser; premium community notes cost PTS. Every download is tracked.
05
Upload notes. A note is fingerprinted, reviewed by an AI moderator, and paid out (+50 PTS) when approved.
06
Earn PTS from uploads, downloads, upvotes, referrals, streaks and daily challenges.
07
Spend PTS to unlock other students’ premium notes (the uploader keeps 50%) or buy cosmetics in the shop.
08
Climb. Weekly and all-time leaderboards for the country, each subject, and each school.
THE LOOP Upload → get approved → earn PTS → unlock better notes → upload more.

Daily challenges & streaks

Three challenges rotate each day (study, upload, upvote, show up). Login streaks pay more each consecutive day.

Leaderboards & badges

Boards for global / subject / school, weekly and all-time. Achievements unlock automatically and never cost points.

04 — Notes & moderation06

Quality on the way in, protection on the way out.

Every upload is reviewed

Uploads are scanned by an AI moderator (plus a hard rule set) for spam, junk, and exam-paper/leaked material. Approvals pay out; rejections tell the uploader why. Nothing risky is auto-approved.

Provenance & copyright

Each file gets a SHA-256 fingerprint at upload, and exact duplicates are rejected. Uploaders keep their copyright and can share a public provenance certificate as proof they made it first. A “Report stolen” button opens a takedown review.

Official notes (written by the EduRank team)

Hermes produces original, CAPS-aligned notes with a PDF; they’re published through a script and marked OFFICIAL. They’re free and public, which is what lets Google index them.

05 — People & roles07

Four kinds of people, plus you.

RoleWhat they can do
StudentBrowse and unlock notes for their grade, upload, earn PTS, climb boards.
TeacherBeyond student access: free viewing of notes in their assigned subjects at their school, and they can mark notes VERIFIED. Joined by invitation from a principal.
PrincipalA dashboard for their school: students, stats, the school’s notes, promotions (who repeats a year), and teacher invites. Joined by invitation from the owner.
Owner (you)Sees everything: people, content, economy, activity. Super-admin over every panel.

Staff are invited, never self-declared

Roles can’t be chosen at signup — that stops anyone claiming to be a teacher. The owner creates a principal invite for a school; that principal creates teacher invites (scoped to subjects). Each invite is a single-use link that expires in 30 days.

INVITE FLOW Owner → principal link → principal → teacher links → teachers.

06 — Getting found on Google08

A free library that Google can read.

Most of the app sits behind a login, which search engines can’t see. The official notes are different: they have public, server-rendered pages built specifically to be found.

What was set up

  • Public pages at /study, /study/<grade>/<subject> and each note.
  • Real text in the HTML (not hidden behind scripts) so crawlers can read the notes.
  • Structured data (schema.org LearningResource + breadcrumbs) for rich results.
  • Sitemap listing every page, and a robots.txt that invites Google into /study.
  • Per-page titles, descriptions and a social share image.
Next for SEO

Verify the site in Google Search Console and submit the sitemap so the notes get indexed. That’s the one step that still needs you.

08 — Security & the law10

Looked after, and POPIA-aware.

Security measures in place

  • Passwords are stored as salted hashes; sessions use unguessable tokens.
  • Private data is scoped — students can’t see other students’ data or other grades.
  • Rate limiting and Turnstile block brute-force and bot signups.
  • Email verification is required before earning or spending.
  • Security headers (HTTPS/HSTS, frame protection) are set across the site.
  • The API has been tested against common attacks (injection, unauthorised access, traversal).

POPIA & legal (South Africa)

  • Privacy Policy with an Information Officer, lawful basis, retention, and data-subject rights.
  • Terms of Service, Cookie policy, Community Guidelines, Copyright/takedown and Accessibility pages.
  • Data is processed on Cloudflare; cross-border handling is disclosed.

These policies are a strong, honest starting point but should be reviewed by a South African attorney before you rely on them commercially.
